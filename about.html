About PennJets

Founded in 2025, PennJets is a private aviation sales and consulting firm. We connect clients with the right aircraft through straightforward advice and personal service.

Our Story

Joseph Pennella founded Penn Jets LLC in 2025. He brokers whole-aircraft and fractional transactions, arranges charter through certificated operators, and advises owners on acquisition strategy. He publishes market studies on specific models and segments: how many airframes exist, how many are actually available, and what that means for a buyer's timeline.

Whether you're buying your first light jet, looking at fractional ownership, or selling a legacy aircraft, PennJets provides the expertise and negotiation power to get the deal done and protect your interests.
